THE LCA TEAM

Different parties.
One national team.

Ten candidates, each with a plan shaped by the place they call home.

01

VICE PRESIDENT · National ticket

Alan Bluespan III

“The citizen, not the system, should be the protagonist.”

An economist and entrepreneur from Chasmia, Alan brings a reformer’s discipline and a builder’s optimism to the national ticket. His 2064 campaign is centered on constitutional government, economic renewal and restoring confidence that public institutions work for citizens.

Constitutional governmentA more competitive economyCaprica’s place in Columbia
02

AMBROSIA · Candidate for MP

Alistair Foulke-McKeon

“Ambrosia should never have to choose between its way of life and a prosperous future.”

Alistair’s plan backs family farms, clean water and the young people who want to make their future in Ambrosia. He is campaigning for reliable freight links, practical apprenticeships and rural services that respect the distance between communities.

Farm export corridorsClean rural waterRegional apprenticeships
Visit Alistair’s Ambrosia campaign →
03

CHASMIA · Candidate for MP

Patrick Cutter

“Chasmia built Caprica. Now we will build its next generation of industry.”

A former Prime Minister and experienced business leader, Patrick is focused on advanced manufacturing, shipbuilding and skilled work. He wants Chasmia’s research institutions, energy producers and industrial firms working from one long-term regional growth plan.

Advanced manufacturingResearch partnershipsEnergy security
04

MYRATI · Candidate for MP

JB Stoner

“Prosperity must travel beyond the waterfront, and power must travel back to Myrati.”

JB is campaigning for local autonomy, better connections and a tourism economy whose success reaches every community. His program would keep more visitor revenue on the islands while improving transport, skills and opportunities beyond Port Aaron.

Island transportLocal decision-makingSkills and tourism
05

OPLANA · Candidate for MP

Tony Blair

“The future belongs to those with the confidence to shape it.”

Tony brings national experience, an outward-looking outlook and a determined focus on practical results to Oplana. He is campaigning to modernize public services, strengthen the region’s growing businesses and invest responsibly in the skills, connections and coastal resilience that Langston needs for the decades ahead.

Modern public servicesEnterprise and skillsCoastal resilience
06

RENO · Candidate for MP

Roberto Libero

“Keep Reno moving, keep it safe, and keep opportunity within reach.”

Roberto champions the entrepreneurs, commuters and families who make Reno one of Caprica’s most dynamic communities. He is focused on safer neighborhoods, better links to Montiablo and more housing choices close to jobs, schools and everyday services.

Faster commutesSafer neighborhoodsHousing choice
07

MONTIABLO · Candidate for MP

Ronan Hayward

“A great capital opens doors, it does not put up barriers.”

An experienced reformer and former Mayor-Governor, Ronan Hayward brings a record of liberal reform and a clear plan for the capital to the LCA team. He is campaigning for homes around transit, open digital government and a city economy that remains active, safe and welcoming after dark.

Homes near transitOpen, digital governmentA thriving night-time economy
08

CAMBRIA · Candidate for MP

Pepe Marti Rutte

“Cambria should be a place where a good idea can become a good living.”

Pepe is standing for a more enterprising, connected and affordable Cambria. His campaign is focused on opening doors for young workers and local firms, expanding practical training, and making it easier to build homes near jobs and transport. He wants national government to give Cambrian communities the tools to grow on their own strengths.

Skills for young workersBacking local enterpriseHomes near transport
09

KALAHOOSKA · Candidate for MP

Rupert Pagi Shaw

“Kalahooska deserves a strong voice in the room where national decisions are made.”

Rupert is campaigning to connect Kalahooska’s communities to opportunity without losing the character that makes the region distinct. His priorities are dependable transport, resilient local services and a practical settlement that gives regional leaders more authority over the decisions closest to home.

Regional transportResilient local servicesDecisions closer to home
10

PLEUCADEUC · Candidate for MP

Mathieu Jeon

“A new generation should be able to build its future in Pleucadeuc.”

Mathieu brings a generational perspective to the LCA team and a focused program for Pleucadeuc. He is campaigning for attainable housing, modern technical education and faster digital connections, so young people and growing firms can choose the region with confidence.

Attainable housingTechnical educationDigital connectivity
