THE LCA TEAM

Different parties.
One national team.

Ten candidates, each with a plan shaped by the place they call home.

01

VICE PRESIDENT · National ticket

Alan Bluespan III

“The citizen, not the system, should be the protagonist.”

An economist, entrepreneur and reformer from Chasmia, Alan enters 2064 with a record of opening utilities to competition, strengthening the national credit rating and defending constitutional order through the courts. His next program joins disciplined public finances with new housing, modern transport and energy investment, while setting consistent national standards for accountable policing.

Constitutional governmentCompetition and sound financesHousing and infrastructure
02

AMBROSIA · Candidate for MP

Alistair Foulke-McKeon

“Recovery was the beginning. Now Ambrosia must grow.”

Alistair is standing on four years of work to renew water systems, strengthen agriculture and rebuild the infrastructure rural communities depend on. His next plan takes Ambrosia from recovery to growth, opening new export markets, supporting local food processing, extending dependable rural broadband and protecting Mount Ambrosia as both a natural inheritance and a source of sustainable opportunity.

Farm exports and local processingClean water and rural broadbandMount Ambrosia conservation

03

CHASMIA · Candidate for MP

Patrick Cutter

“We cannot, and we will not, go back.”

Chasmia’s MP since 2058, Patrick previously served as Prime Minister and later Deputy Prime Minister. His regional plan starts with dependable roads and infrastructure, then builds on Chasmia’s agricultural strength through smart irrigation and modern farm technology. He is also campaigning for specialist healthcare closer to home, stronger mountain tourism and skilled jobs from wind and hydroelectric power.

Roads and rural infrastructureHigh-tech agricultureHealthcare and clean energy
Visit Patrick’s Chasmia campaign →
04

MYRATI · Candidate for MP

JB Stoner

“We export Myratian goods, and import opportunities.”

JB offers steady leadership after years in which Myrati chose recovery over political violence. Through the Prosper Myrati program, he is working to turn Port Aaron into a commercial gateway with deep-water berths, shipbuilding and repair, better road and rail links, an expanded airport and Free Trade Port status. His plan also supports students who study on the mainland and bring their skills home.

Port Aaron and free tradeAirport, road and rail linksPeace, education and opportunity
Visit JB’s Myrati campaign →
05

OPLANA · Candidate for MP

Tony Blair

“A safer, freer Oplana, easier to build in, better to live in.”

Tony is standing for practical optimism in Oplana, firm action against gangs alongside a credible route out of addiction and violence. His plan combines visible neighborhood patrols and accountable safety audits with a new recovery hub, homes on derelict land, safer rentals and a Market Mile reset that cuts permit costs for local traders. Every school leaver should reach a job, apprenticeship or training place within six months.

Safe streets and recoveryHomes on derelict landLocal business and apprenticeships
Visit Tony’s Oplana campaign →
06

RENO · Candidate for MP

Roberto Libero

“Keep Reno moving, keep it safe, and keep opportunity within reach.”

Roberto champions the entrepreneurs, commuters and families who make Reno one of Caprica’s most dynamic communities. He is focused on safer neighborhoods, better links to Montiablo and more housing choices close to jobs, schools and everyday services.

Faster commutesSafer neighborhoodsHousing choice
07

MONTIABLO · Candidate for MP

Ronan Hayward

“This city does not need a spectator.”

An experienced reformer and former Mayor-Governor, Ronan is campaigning to make the capital work as one connected region. He backs a single CATA network and fare across bus, tram, metro, ferry and regional rail, firm public oversight of the New Homerville energy project, and a one-stop launch lane for small firms and cultural venues. Public delivery dashboards would show residents exactly what government has promised and completed.

One network, one fareAffordable power with oversightOpen government and enterprise
Visit Ronan’s Montiablo campaign →
08

CAMBRIA · Candidate for MP

Pepe Marti Rutte

“Cambria should be a place where a good idea can become a good living.”

Pepe is standing for a more enterprising, connected and affordable Cambria. His campaign is focused on opening doors for young workers and local firms, expanding practical training, and making it easier to build homes near jobs and transport. He wants national government to give Cambrian communities the tools to grow on their own strengths.

Skills for young workersBacking local enterpriseHomes near transport
09

KALAHOOSKA · Candidate for MP

Rupert Pagi Shaw

“Kalahooska deserves a strong voice in the room where national decisions are made.”

Rupert is campaigning to connect Kalahooska’s communities to opportunity without losing the character that makes the region distinct. His priorities are dependable transport, resilient local services and a practical settlement that gives regional leaders more authority over the decisions closest to home.

Regional transportResilient local servicesDecisions closer to home
Visit Rupert’s Kalahooska campaign →
10

PLEUCADEUC · Candidate for MP

Mathieu Jeon

“Staying home should never mean standing still.”

Born and raised in Pleucadeuc, Mathieu sees representation as a service and wants his generation to build ambitious lives without leaving its roots behind. His central commitment is a full high-speed upgrade for the Valcartier, Fiévreau and Syon corridor, coordinated with local rail and buses. The investment would support Caprican apprenticeships, housing near stations and a strong voice for Pleucadeuc’s Gallic-speaking communities.

Southern high-speed railLocal connections and housingGallic life and opportunity
