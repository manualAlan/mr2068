A personal belief

“Growth should leave a place stronger than it found it.”

Kalahooska taught me that stewardship and enterprise are partners. A well managed forest provides work today because somebody cared about tomorrow. The same principle should guide public life.

I want young people to inherit real choices here: a skilled job, a home within reach and services that do not disappear with the next budget cycle. Local people understand this land. National government should trust that knowledge and keep its promises.

Kalahooska · 2068 to 2072

The things that should
change in everyday life.

A regional programme tied to the Alliance’s national commitments. Open each proposal for the delivery test behind it.

01 / Working forests

More value from every tree.

Our natural wealth should support skilled work and long term stewardship close to home.

Move up the value chain+

Support open investment in engineered timber, furniture, biomass byproducts and modern mills where demand and sustainable supply support a business case. Connect firms to equipment expensing and export certification.

Harvest, restore, repeat+

Set transparent regional forest plans based on independent inventories. Require replanting, habitat protection and funded restoration, with results published by catchment and concession.

Open contracts to local firms+

Publish forestry, road and public building pipelines early. Break suitable contracts into accessible lots and enforce prompt payment without weakening safety or value tests.

02 / Connections

Distance should not decide your future.

A failed bridge or unreliable signal can isolate a family, a clinic and a business at once.

Keep essential roads open+

Publish a condition audit of bridges, slopes and freight routes, then fund maintenance by safety and isolation risk. Show the responsible agency and next milestone.

Reliable digital access+

Tender support only where the market cannot provide service, paying for tested speed and reliability. Keep telephone and in person access to essential public services.

Transport built around real journeys+

Coordinate regional services around work, school and medical appointments. Use competitive essential route contracts where demand cannot sustain a commercial service.

03 / Local power

Decisions closer to the people living with them.

Regional authority must come with the revenue and responsibility to make it real.

A practical regional settlement+

Seek multi year powers over local transport, skills and land management, with funding responsibilities written into law and independently audited.

Keep project value here+

Return a transparent share of royalties from nationally significant resource projects to host communities for durable infrastructure and skills.

Open government by default+

Publish contracts, land decisions and delivery dashboards in usable form, with assisted access for residents who are offline. Protect personal casework.

04 / A life here

A reason to stay. A reason to return.

Local pride lasts when people can build an independent life around it.

Homes where services can support them+

Plan suitable housing in existing towns alongside water, schools and transport. Invite smaller builders to compete and publish approval times.

Skills tied to a real job+

Build paid pathways in forestry, engineering, care and construction with shared apprenticeship administration for small employers.

Care within reach+

Coordinate visiting clinics, primary care and patient transport, and publish regional access times. Support unpaid caregivers through the national respite commitment.

The standard I set for myself

Kalahooska will be heard, and every promise made here will be visible.

I will hold regular constituency sessions across the region, publish a quarterly account of these commitments and tell you which institution is responsible when progress stalls.

I will put costs, funding and long term maintenance beside every major proposal. You should be able to judge the work, not just hear the announcement.
