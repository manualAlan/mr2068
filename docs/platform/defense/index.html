Defense

Sea control, air reach and a smaller elite army built for an island nation.

SEA / AIR / MOBILITY

An island force designed around the map.

Caprica does not need the largest military in Columbia. It needs the force most able to keep hostile power away from its shores, reinforce CU partners and move decisively when deterrence fails.

01

Command the approaches

The navy will become the senior service for an island nation dependent on open water.

  • More submarines, escorts and unmanned maritime patrols
  • Distributed anti-ship and air-defense batteries
  • A protected logistics network across Caprican ports
02

Own the air picture

A stronger air force will connect surveillance, air defense and long-range mobility.

  • Persistent maritime patrol and airborne early warning
  • Hardened and dispersed operating bases
  • Strategic lift for national and CU contingencies
03

A smaller elite army

Shift from mass formations to a mobile force able to deploy quickly across islands, ports and allied territory.

  • Lean expeditionary brigades with integrated drones
  • Larger trained reserves and faster mobilization
  • Protected communications down to small-unit level
04

Buy readiness, not announcements

Procurement will be judged by equipment available for deployment and crews trained to use it.

  • A ten-year funded capability plan
  • Quarterly readiness reporting to Parliament
  • Open competition with sovereign maintenance capacity

A freer, safer, more capable Caprica.
