Foreign Policy

Liberal in purpose, realist in method and dependable in the Columbian Union.

THE BLUESPAN DOCTRINE

Open to the world. Clear about our interests.

Caprican foreign policy will be liberal in purpose and realist in method: defending the republic, keeping the sea lanes open and sustaining reliable partners without turning every injustice abroad into an unlimited military mission.

01

A stronger Columbian Union

Caprica prospers when the CU is secure, competitive and able to act together where interests genuinely converge.

  • Complete the CU market in services and digital trade
  • Mutual resilience plans for energy, medicines and critical technology
  • Collective defense commitments that are specific and funded
02

Gateway Caprica

Use our ports, law, finance and geography to connect Columbia with Albeuman and the wider world.

  • Trusted-trader corridors through Caprican ports
  • Commercial diplomacy focused on investment and market access
  • Open-science and student agreements with reliable partners
03

Peace through balance

Diplomacy works best when commitments are credible and objectives are limited.

  • No open-ended wars of political conversion
  • Support territorial integrity, including Kerevan’s restoration
  • Keep Oshmit isolated from weapons and dual-use supply chains
04

Build on the Kaoqing Communique

Normalization with OURS showed that persistent diplomacy can reduce danger without surrendering principle.

  • A permanent Twin Strait crisis hotline
  • Maritime deconfliction and incident-reporting rules
  • Parliamentary votes for deployments beyond immediate defense
