THE ALLIANCE

Agreement where it matters.

LCA unites three distinct political traditions behind one program for the 2064 election.

We will govern from common ground, protect constitutional democracy, reward work and enterprise, deliver dependable public services, and give every region a genuine stake in national progress. Our parties retain their identities. Our candidates share one commitment, a capable, open and forward-looking Caprica.

WORK, TAX AND ENTERPRISE

Let effort pay again.

Lower the tax on work, back small business and put competition before political control.

Raise the tax-free threshold, simplify business taxes and introduce a one-in, two-out rule for costly new regulation. Independent regulators, not ministers, will protect fair prices and open markets.

LCA will

  • Raise the personal tax-free threshold and index it to wages
  • Create one simple quarterly return for small firms and sole traders
  • Require every new regulation to publish its cost to households and business
  • Open government purchasing to young firms and regional suppliers
  • Protect independent competition enforcement in concentrated markets
02

JUSTICE, POLICING AND DEFENSE

Order without overreach.

Visible policing at home and dependable allies across the Columbian community.

Put more officers where people see them, target organized crime and cyber threats, and give courts the capacity to move cases quickly. Abroad, we will remain a reliable Columbian ally and invest in modern defense.

LCA will

  • Put neighborhood teams on visible, multi-year staffing plans
  • Create specialist national units for organized crime, fraud and cyber threats
  • Fund additional court capacity and digital case management
  • Guarantee independent review of emergency and surveillance powers
  • Publish a ten-year defense capability and procurement plan
03

HOUSING, TRANSPORT AND WATER

From delay to delivery.

Faster homes, better transport and reliable energy, without years lost to paperwork.

Create a single approvals pathway for nationally significant projects, unlock housing near transport, and invite competitive investment into rail, ports, water and clean firm power.

LCA will

  • Set binding decision deadlines for major planning applications
  • Allow more homes around rapid transit and established town centers
  • Create regional infrastructure agreements with transparent milestones
  • Use competitive delivery contracts for rail, ports and water renewal
  • Publish a national pipeline so builders can invest in people and equipment
04

HEALTH, EDUCATION AND SKILLS

A guarantee of outcomes.

Judge public services by the result for you, not the size of the department.

Guarantee timely primary care, publish school and hospital outcomes, expand technical pathways and let public, community and independent providers compete to deliver quality care.

LCA will

  • Introduce a primary-care access guarantee with regional reporting
  • Publish comparable waiting-time and treatment-outcome dashboards
  • Protect core literacy and numeracy time in every primary school
  • Build employer-designed technical academies in all major regions
  • Fund services around the citizen, whether delivery is public or independent
05

ENERGY AND THE ENVIRONMENT

Abundance is climate policy.

Build a resilient electricity system that cuts emissions without rationing growth.

Replace stop-start intervention with a technology-neutral clean power market. Speed up grids, storage and generation while protecting landscapes through clear national standards.

LCA will

  • Create a single national connection process for new generation
  • Run long-term auctions for clean, firm capacity and storage
  • Modernize the grid before congestion becomes a growth tax
  • Price pollution consistently and return proceeds through lower taxes
  • Pay host communities a share of major energy-project revenues
06

FARMS, ISLANDS AND REGIONS

One country, many strengths.

Give regions the tools and revenue to shape their own growth.

Move practical decisions closer to communities, secure clean rural water and connect producers, islands and smaller cities to national and Columbian markets.

LCA will

  • Give regions multi-year transport and skills budgets
  • Create a clean-water renewal fund with public quality reporting
  • Upgrade farm-to-port freight corridors and cold-chain capacity
  • Give Myrati greater authority over tourism and local transport
  • Share royalties from nationally significant projects with host regions
07

DEMOCRACY AND GOVERNMENT

Open by default.

Protect constitutional government and make the public sector radically easier to see and use.

Rights, checks and honest administration are not obstacles to action. They are how lasting reform earns consent. LCA will pair a capable state with real limits on political power.

LCA will

  • Publish contracts, performance data and ministerial meetings by default
  • Create one secure digital front door for national services
  • Require sunset votes for emergency powers
  • Strengthen independent fiscal and electoral institutions
  • Give citizens a right to a timely decision from public agencies
08

TRADE AND COLUMBIA

Open, allied, prepared.

Use Caprica’s commercial strength and Columbian ties to keep the country secure and prosperous.

Caprica should be a dependable Columbian ally, a champion of open trade and a practical partner across the Aortic Sea. Economic security and national security will be planned together.

LCA will

  • Deepen the Columbian single market in services and digital trade
  • Create secure supply agreements for medicines, energy and critical technology
  • Expand port capacity and trusted-trader lanes
  • Coordinate cyber defense with Columbian partners
  • Tie development partnerships to transparent, measurable outcomes
