Housing

A place to call yours.

A home gives you somewhere to begin. Finding one should leave room for the rest of your life.

↓

WHAT WE BELIEVE

We believe a place of your own should be an ordinary ambition in Caprica.

A home changes what you can plan. You can take a job, start a family, settle near friends or choose a quieter life. When every spare aurum goes on keeping a roof overhead, those choices shrink. Too many Capricans are being asked to wait for their own lives to begin.

We want builders to build and communities to grow with confidence. Clear rules, working infrastructure and more homes will give people real choice. Ownership should be within reach. Renting should feel secure. Both deserve a serious place in the country we are building.

A home should make your world bigger.

Make room

A growing neighborhood can welcome new people and keep the things residents love. Good streets, schools and transport belong in the plan from the beginning. Saying yes to homes means taking the whole place seriously.

Respect the person renting

A rental is somebody's home. People deserve safe rooms, repairs that happen and a contract they can plan around. Clear rights and fair dispute resolution help responsible tenants and landlords get on with their lives.

Open the door to ownership

Saving for a deposit should lead somewhere. More supply must come first, with carefully limited help for working households buying additional new homes. Public support should make the market more accessible instead of pushing prices higher.

OUR 2068 COMMITMENTS

What gets built next

A practical plan, with standards to meet and choices made in public.

50% more homes by 2072+

Increase annual completions by 50% against the independently verified 2068 total. Allow quality homes within 800 meters of rapid transit and in town centers, including homes above shops. Regional agreements will match new homes with water, power, schools and transport. Publish monthly starts, completions and costs.

A decision within 120 days+

Complete applications in growth zones should receive a decision within 120 days. Compliant infill using approved designs gets 60 days. Safety, flood and habitat rules still apply. A missed deadline brings a fee refund and an independent appeal, so silence cannot hold an application indefinitely.

A rental you can settle into+

Introduce lasting contracts, portable deposits and enforceable standards for warmth, safety and repairs. A specialist tribunal will aim to resolve disputes within 30 days. Publish local rents and vacancies so communities can see where more homes are needed and whether the shortage is easing.

Ownership help that adds homes+

Offer portable housing savings accounts and a capped shared ownership scheme for eligible working households buying additional new homes. Publish income limits, resale terms and subsidy costs before allocating places. Support must add supply and pass independent fiscal checks, not bid up the price of homes already in short supply.

Services ready when the keys arrive+

Agree ten years of local water, power, school and transport capacity through regional growth compacts. A revolving Infrastructure Capacity Fund will advance independently appraised investment, repaid through disclosed development levies and gains in land value. Include maintenance and test the plan against lower land receipts before borrowing.

THE DELIVERY TEST

Judge the promise by the result.

What happens first, what is measured, and how it is paid for.

First step
Verify the 2068 housing total within 100 days. Put growth compacts and planning clocks in place by 2069.
Public test
Annual completions 50% above 2068 by 2072. Publish monthly construction figures and local rent and vacancy data.
Funding rule
Cost ownership support before allocating places. Match infrastructure advances to a tested repayment plan, with no receipt counted twice.
