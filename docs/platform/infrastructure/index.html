WHAT WE BELIEVE

We believe the freedom to build needs foundations that hold.

A home is not ready without water and power. A job is not within reach without a dependable journey. A business cannot grow on a promise of a connection someday. Infrastructure turns an opportunity on paper into something people can actually use.

Our approach is practical: maintain what works, build where the need is proven and publish the whole bill. Government should set dependable rules, buy openly and name who is responsible. Private investment should compete to meet clear standards, with public obligations kept in plain sight.

The next connection should come with a date.

Maintain before it fails

A repaired pipe or bridge may never make a grand opening. It still matters. Asset condition, repair backlogs and maintenance funding belong alongside new construction in every serious plan.

Buy reliability

Cheap electricity is little use when it is unavailable. Compare energy options on their full cost, dependable capacity and verified emissions. Let technologies compete against the same demanding test.

Make the plan public

People should know what will be built, when it will work and who must explain a delay. Show operating and financing costs alongside construction, including the obligations passed to future taxpayers and bill payers.

OUR 2068 COMMITMENTS

Connections with commitments

A practical plan, with standards to meet and choices made in public.

Reliable power, honest bills+

Let storage, demand response, geothermal, advanced nuclear and other dependable clean options compete on cost, reliability and emissions. Publish guaranteed capacity, backup costs and consumer obligations. Keep the binding emissions cap and return at least half of actual auction receipts to households equally and transparently.

Grid connections with dates+

Prioritize ready projects over speculative reservations. Publish connection dates and charges for homes, ports and industry, with independent appeals and cost checks. Before buying small modular reactors, require independent regulation and funded waste and decommissioning plans. Compare alternatives over their full working lives.

A joined-up journey+

Agree an automatic weekly fare cap across participating rail, bus and ferry networks, with cash or assisted options and revenue sharing settled first. Upgrade the southern rail corridor in independently costed stages, subject to funding and passenger demand. Connect stations to local services and publish operating subsidies.

Clean water and dependable digital service+

Publish water quality tests, leakage and repair schedules for every service area. Protect a public minimum for maintenance in transport and water capital budgets. Tender digital support competitively for underserved areas with open wholesale access, actual reliability reports and backup connections for critical services.

Every major project must earn its place+

Projects above 1 billion aurums need an options appraisal, reference design, cost and benefit ranges, a named authority and funded maintenance. Independent reviews release money in stages. Publish contract changes, overruns and guarantees, with proportionate checks for smaller projects too.

THE DELIVERY TEST

Judge the promise by the result.

What happens first, what is measured, and how it is paid for.

First step
By 2069, publish the national investment pipeline, energy auction terms and grid connection dates. Agree regional rollout milestones for transport and water.
Public test
Quarterly reports on bills, reliability, emissions and dividends. By 2070, shared fare agreements and public reports on network condition.
Funding rule
Independently appraise capital borrowing and disclose lifetime costs. Keep maintenance funded; do not use variable emissions receipts for permanent payroll.
